Meet Mark

A practice shaped by patience.

I’m a quilt maker drawn to pieces that feel deeply useful and quietly expressive.

Blending traditional quilt-making techniques with a modern editorial eye, I create heirloom pieces designed to hold your stories. My hope is that my quilts bring a sense of warmth, intention, and grounding to your everyday spaces.
