Meet Mark

A lifelong love of sewing.

I create pieces that feel useful and expressive.

I have been surrounded by sewing and quilting since I was very young. My mother was a talented seamstress, and the grandmothers and aunts on both sides of my family were avid quilters. Before I was old enough for school, my maternal grandmother, Meme, looked after me while my mother worked. By the time I was five, she had taught me how to cook, sew, cross-stitch, and embroider. I still remember being the exact height of their quilting frame, watching in fascination as the needles were pushed up and down by my grandmother and aunts.

Fast-forward forty years, and I finally bought a secondhand sewing machine of my own. My first projects were simple and fun—hemming pants and adding darts to shirts. But when the pandemic hit and my massage business essentially came to a halt, sewing became my lifeline. I started making face masks—over 600 in total—and that is how I survived.

Today, my skills have grown and my projects have increased in complexity. I truly love to sew, focusing mostly on taking thrifted clothing and fabric and transforming them into beautiful, practical, and decorative items.
