The Process

Decorative Quilts for Quiet Spaces

Mark approaches decorative quilts as pieces that shape the feeling of a room as much as they add warmth or texture. The process begins with scale, palette, and placement, considering how the work will live alongside light, furniture, and everyday rhythms.

Whether designed as a wall hanging, a draped accent, or a softer visual focal point, each piece is made with balance and restraint in mind. The goal is to create something artful and lasting, bringing beauty, calm, and character into the spaces people return to most.
